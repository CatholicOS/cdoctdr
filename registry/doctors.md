# Doctors of the Church

Canonical draft IDs for the 38 Doctors of the Church, from the four great Latin Doctors proclaimed in 1298 to St. John Henry Newman (2025), in declaration order. `#` is the declaration-sequence position; the ID is the Doctor's Latin nominative lemma (aligned with the Roman Martyrology). **Declared by** is the proclaiming pope as a [CRPDR](../../crpdr) `rp:` ID; **Eulogy** is the [CRMEDR](../../crmedr) `mr:` cross-reference to the memorial eulogy of the Roman Martyrology (empty where the saint is not in the editio typica altera 2004). All IDs are drafts pending CETF review ([schema proposal](../docs/schema-proposal.md)).
